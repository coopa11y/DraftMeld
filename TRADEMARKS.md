# DraftMeld trademark policy

DraftMeld's source code is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0. That license does not grant rights to misrepresent a modified build or third-party service as an official DraftMeld release.

You may use the DraftMeld name truthfully to:

- refer to the project;
- say that your software is based on or compatible with DraftMeld;
- link to the project or discuss it;
- identify an unmodified community image obtained from the project's official registry.

If you distribute a modified build or operate a third-party service, clearly identify who made and supports it. Do not use the DraftMeld name, logo, or visual identity in a way that suggests sponsorship, certification, or official status without written permission. A descriptive phrase such as “Based on DraftMeld” is preferred for forks.

The phrases “Official DraftMeld,” “Official supported build,” and similar claims are reserved for packages and services published or explicitly authorized by the DraftMeld maintainers.

This policy is intended to protect users from confusion while preserving normal open-source use. Open an issue if a proposed use is unclear.
