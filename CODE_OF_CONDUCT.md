# Code of conduct

DraftMeld contributors are expected to create a welcoming, harassment-free community.

Be respectful, critique ideas rather than people, assume good intent while addressing harmful behavior, and make room for participants with different experience levels and backgrounds. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior is inconsistent with these expectations. Conduct concerns should be reported privately to the maintainers rather than posted publicly.
